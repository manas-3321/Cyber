## 4. Root-cause analysis

Internet-exposed SSH on web-srv-02.corp.local accepted password authentication. Attackers at 203.0.113.44 guessed valid credentials, authenticated successfully and escalated privileges with sudo. The absence of MFA and of source-IP rate limiting allowed the brute force to succeed.

The chain is reconstructable end-to-end from normalised telemetry: each alert below is linked to the exact event IDs listed in the evidence section, and the timeline is ordered by the original source timestamps rather than by detection time.

## 5. Impact assessment

- **Brute Force:** Credential compromise risk for the targeted accounts. If the attacker authenticated, confidentiality of data reachable by that account is impacted and further persistence is likely. Business impact is high when the account has administrative rights.
- **Persistence:** The adversary retains access after remediation unless the persistence mechanism is removed; integrity of the host configuration is impacted.
- **Malware:** Confidentiality and integrity impact on the infected endpoint and any credentials or sessions present on it. Command-and-control traffic indicates an active adversary.
- **High-value assets involved:** web-srv-02.corp.local

## 8. Recommendations

- Disable password-based SSH in favour of keys or short-lived certificates; add fail2ban/CrowdSec.
- Alert on successful authentication from any IP with prior failures (rule DET-002).
- Move internet-facing administration behind a VPN or zero-trust access proxy.
- Block Office child-process creation via ASR rules (Win32/Office / PowerShell).
- Enforce PowerShell script block logging and AMSI telemetry to the SIEM.
- Drive EDR coverage to 100% of endpoints with tamper protection enabled.
- Enforce phishing-resistant MFA on all remote access paths (VPN, SSH, RDP, webmail).
- Segment server, workstation and OT networks; restrict east-west SMB and RDP to jump hosts.
- Reduce detection latency by streaming endpoint and firewall telemetry with < 5 minute delay.
- Run a quarterly tabletop exercise against this playbook and update contact trees.
- Track mean time to detect/respond as a SOC KPI and review monthly with the CISO.

## 9. Lessons learned and detection improvements

- Password authentication on internet-facing SSH is the root enabler; MFA or key-only auth removes it.
- The threshold rule fired after 8 failures, but a successful login was only correlated 14 minutes later - move DET-002 to real-time streaming.
- Add geo-velocity and impossible-travel correlation for successful logins.
